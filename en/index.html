01 · review

One review. Three decisions it unblocks.

The contracts may already be under a formal audit — or headed for one. park13 looks at whether the system holds under real use: ledgers, retries, RPC concentration, silent failures, and the paths operators actually run. Same method for pre-audit prep, ship decisions, or residual risk after findings.

00

Pre-audit review

Surface architectural, implementation, and operational risks before formal audit time starts. Separate fix-now items from questions that belong with the audit firm.

01

Go / no-go

02

Post-audit residual risk

Open findings, code changes, compensating controls, and operational risk — turned into an executable release decision.

Formal assurance stays with the independent audit firm. When useful, remediation and re-verification stay tied to written finding IDs and a fixed scope.

02 · scope

Not only the contracts — what fails around them.

Audit firms already cover a lot of contract surface. The edge here is the rest of the path: whether books diverge from chain state, what a stuck nonce does to retries, how concentrated RPC really is, and which failures stay silent.

One integration or digital-asset system, end to end — deep enough for the decision, not a generic company-wide survey.

03 · report

The report ends in a decision, not a vulnerability list.

  • Verdict: GREEN, AMBER, or RED for the intended use
  • Scope: what was reviewed and what was not
  • Ranked findings (e.g. P13-04): failure mode, severity, who or what is hurt
  • Trust boundaries: keys, admins, upgrades, custody, bridges, external dependencies
  • Split list: fix before audit or launch · leave for the formal audit · defer
  • Change triggers: what would move the verdict
  • Executive brief: full technical report in English or Japanese; short summary in the other language when useful
