02 · review

One review. Three decisions it unblocks.

The same method applies whether you are preparing for a formal audit, deciding to ship, or resolving findings after an audit. The output is what can fail, what changes the verdict, and what should happen next.

00

Pre-audit review

Identify architectural, implementation, and operational risks before expensive audit time begins. Separate what should be fixed now from the specialist questions that belong in the formal audit.

01

Go / no-go

Decide whether a named system is fit to ship, integrate, or approve under its intended use and operating conditions.

02

Post-audit residual risk

Turn open findings, code changes, compensating controls, and operational risk into an executable release decision.

Formal assurance remains with the independent audit firm. When useful, remediation and re-verification remain tied to written finding IDs and a fixed scope.

04 · output

The report ends in a decision, not a vulnerability list.

  • Verdict: GREEN, AMBER, or RED for the intended use
  • Scope: what was reviewed and what was not
  • Ranked findings: failure mode, severity, and affected assets, users, or operations
  • Trust boundaries: keys, administrators, upgrades, custody, bridges, and external dependencies
  • Split list: fix before audit or launch, reserve for the formal audit, or defer
  • Change triggers: evidence or implementation changes required to improve the verdict
  • Executive brief: technical report in English or Japanese, with a summary in the other language where useful

The report supports a decision for the agreed use, assumptions, and scope. It is not a formal security audit, regulatory assurance, or proof that the system is free of vulnerabilities.
