03 · output

The report ends in a decision, not a vulnerability list.

  • Verdict: GREEN, AMBER, or RED for your intended use of X
  • Scope: what was reviewed and what was not
  • Ranked findings: what fails, how badly, and who it hurts
  • Trust boundaries: keys, admins, upgrades, custody, bridges, external dependencies
  • Split list: fix before the audit or launch, versus leave for the firm or defer
  • Change triggers: what would move the verdict
  • Executive brief: a short summary for decision makers, in English or Japanese

05 · fit

Only when X and the decision are clear.

A fit when

  • There is a dated audit, release, integration, approval, or partnership decision
  • The system X (or integration path) can be named
  • Code, technical docs, and owners are reachable in an agreed scope
  • You are willing to change design or implementation when risk warrants it

Not offered

  • "Reviewed by park13" badges or engagements whose only goal is a formal audit certificate
  • Open-ended staff augmentation or retainers
  • Token, NFT, investment, listing, or market strategy work
  • General advice with no named X and no decision

Hardening or re-verification limited to written finding IDs is a separate, capped option.

park13 is a solo practice, not a firm. Engagements run one at a time, so there may be a wait.
